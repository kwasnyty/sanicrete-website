Terms and Conditions

These SMS Terms and Conditions ("Terms") govern the SaniCrete crew schedule text message program operated by Kwasny Company, doing business as SaniCrete ("SaniCrete," "we," "us"). By agreeing to receive these text messages, you agree to these Terms.

1. Program description

SaniCrete texts work schedules to its own field employees. Messages include upcoming job sites, dates and start times, notices when a schedule changes, links to your full schedule, and reminders to confirm your schedule. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

2. How you join

You join by telling a SaniCrete manager, in person or by phone, that you agree to receive work schedule texts at your cell number. Your first text from us confirms that you are signed up. Joining is optional and is not a condition of employment. If you do not join, we will send your schedule by email.

3. Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies with your schedule, usually a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply, based on your mobile plan. SaniCrete does not charge for these messages.

4. How to stop

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will get one final text confirming you are unsubscribed. To rejoin, text START to (734) 550-4418 or tell a SaniCrete manager.

5. Help

Reply HELP to any message, or call Tyler Kwasny at (248) 974-4431.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Give us a mobile number that belongs to you, and tell us if it changes.
  • Reply STOP if you no longer want these texts, or if you give up the number.
  • Confirm your schedule when asked, and contact your manager if anything in a text looks wrong.

7. Service limitations

Text messages may be delayed or not delivered because of carrier, network, phone or coverage issues outside our control. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Your manager, your email and your full schedule remain the official record of your schedule. If a text conflicts with them, they control.
