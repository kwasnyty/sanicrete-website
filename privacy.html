Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Last updated: August 14, 2026

SaniCrete ("we," "us," "our") manufactures and installs resinous and urethane cement flooring for food and beverage processing facilities. This policy explains what information we collect through www.sanicrete.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and quote requests. Name, company, email, phone, facility location, and project details you submit through our forms.
  • Live chat. Messages you send through the chat widget on our site, and any contact details you provide there.
  • Email sign-ups. Your email address if you subscribe to updates or request a promotional offer.
  • Orders. If you purchase a product such as SaniPatch, our payment processor collects the billing and shipping details needed to complete your order.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data. Pages visited, time on site, referring site, approximate location (city or region), browser and device type.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Used for analytics, advertising measurement, and to keep the chat widget working.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16, and our site is intended for business use.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to quote requests, questions, and chat messages
  • Prepare estimates and provide flooring services
  • Process and ship product orders
  • Send marketing or promotional email, only if you asked for it
  • Understand how the site is used so we can improve it
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising
  • Meet legal, tax, and recordkeeping obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Service Providers We Use

We share information only with providers who help us operate. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics
  • Google Ads — advertising and conversion measurement
  • Tidio — live chat
  • Formspree — contact and quote form delivery
  • Mailchimp — email list management and delivery
  • Square — payment processing. Card details go directly to Square; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Microsoft — business email and calendar

We may also disclose information if required by law, or in connection with a business sale or merger.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies for analytics, advertising measurement, and chat functionality. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not work correctly if you do.

You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, and manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings.

Marketing Emails

We send marketing email only to people who opted in. Every marketing message includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect promptly. We may still send you non-marketing messages related to a quote, project, or order you requested.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Opt out of marketing email at any time
  • Ask us not to sell or share your information (we do not sell it)

To make a request, email tylerk@sanicrete.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Data Retention and Security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and we will update the date at the top.
