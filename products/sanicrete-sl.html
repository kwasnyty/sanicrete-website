SaniCrete SL

Self-Leveling Urethane Concrete

SaniCrete SL is a self-leveling, heavy duty, abrasion resistant flooring system based on polyurethane cement technology. Ideal for wet environments, it provides a durable colored quartz textured surface with a non-slip matte finish. The surface texture can be easily customized to suit the environment — our most versatile product.

  • Self-leveling with non-slip matte finish
  • Modifiable surface texture
  • Excellent chemical resistance
  • Thermal shock resistant
  • USDA accepted
  • Fast cure — minimal downtime
  • Eco-friendly, zero VOCs
  • Excellent non-slip coefficient in wet environments
Technical Specifications
Thickness3/8" (1/4" for less demanding applications)
Compressive Strength8,000 psi
FinishNon-slip matte, quartz textured
