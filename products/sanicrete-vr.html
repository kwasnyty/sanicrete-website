SaniCrete VR

Vertical & Cove Base System

SaniCrete VR (Vertical Resin) is composed of water dispersed polyurethane resin combined with very fine graded silica aggregate and cement. It is designed specifically to form a seamless transition between the floor and wall, great for wash down areas. VR is hand trowel applied. It is resistant to abrasion, light impact, chemical attack, thermal shock and other physical aggression. VR can also be used to protect drainage systems, containment pits, curbs and wall finishes.

Key Benefits

  • Good impact and abrasion resistance
  • Eco-friendly, zero VOCs, taint free
  • Unaffected by freeze/thaw cycles
  • Superior for extreme hygiene sterile process areas
  • Stable to high pressure cleaning or continuous hot water up to 235°F
  • Acceptable in USDA inspected facilities
  • Does not contain phthalates
  • Available with permanent antimicrobial protection

Typical Applications

  • Preparation, production, wash down areas
  • Chemical processing
  • Dairies
  • Wineries, distilleries and breweries
  • Secondary containment areas
  • Commercial kitchens
  • Freezers and refrigerated storage
  • Knee wall, concrete curb protection
  • Walls
