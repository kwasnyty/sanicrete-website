SaniCrete PC

Protective Sealcoat & Coating System

SaniCrete PC is a light to medium duty, low viscosity and economical coating system built on the same polyurethane cement technology as our other SaniCrete products — without the coarse aggregates. It provides good abrasion and chemical resistance as a protective sealcoat over SaniCrete MD, SL, STX, HF, or VR. PC is not UV stable; for UV-exposed areas, ask about our SaniThane topcoat for UV stability.

Typically installed at 8-10 mils per coat, SaniCrete PC is ideal for sealing broadcasted flooring systems, priming concrete surfaces, and creating a sanitary lock coat on cove base transitions, curbing, and walls.

Key Benefits

  • Good impact and abrasion resistance
  • Good chemical resistance
  • Fast cure resulting in minimal downtime
  • Eco-friendly, zero VOCs, taint free
  • Acceptable for use in USDA inspected facilities
  • Outstanding bond to SaniCrete broadcasted systems and concrete
  • Does not contain phthalates
  • Available with permanent antimicrobial protection

Typical Applications

  • Seal coating SaniCrete broadcasted systems
  • Priming or scratch coating concrete surfaces
  • Wall, curbing and cove base sealing
  • Sanitary lock coat for SaniCrete cove/floor transitions
  • Dairies
  • Meat, fish and poultry processing areas
  • Freezers and refrigerated storage
