SaniCrete STX

Heavy-Duty Urethane Concrete

Our flagship heavy-duty cementitious urethane flooring system with patented helically twisted stainless steel wire reinforcement. Originally developed for earthquake-resistant structures, STX delivers twice the impact resistance of standard urethane cement.

  • Patented stainless steel wire reinforcement
  • Exceptional thermal shock resistance (-50°F to 250°F)
  • Superior chemical resistance
  • USDA accepted
  • Foot traffic in 4–6 hours
  • Eco-friendly, zero VOCs
