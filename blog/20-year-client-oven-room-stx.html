20-Year Client, Brand New Oven Room

7,000 SF of SaniCrete STX installed in a new oven room for a client who has trusted us for over two decades

Home / Blog / 20-Year Client Oven Room
March 16, 2026 Project Spotlight

The ultimate compliment in this business? When someone moves to a new plant and brings you with them.

We have been working with this guy for over 20 years. That kind of relationship does not happen by accident. It happens because you show up, do the work right, and stand behind it every single time. When he moved to a new facility and needed flooring for a brand new oven room, there was no bid process. He picked up the phone and called us.

The Project: 7,000 SF Oven Room

This was a ground-up oven room installation, not a repair or restoration. New construction means new concrete, a clean substrate, and the opportunity to install the flooring system the right way from day one. No demo, no old coatings to strip, just fresh slab ready for a system that can handle what an oven room throws at it.

Oven rooms are one of the toughest environments in food processing. The floors take constant thermal cycling from ambient temperature to extreme heat and back again. Add in daily washdowns, chemical cleaning agents, and heavy rolling loads from racks and carts, and most flooring systems do not last. That is exactly why SaniCrete STX is the right call.

Why STX for Oven Rooms

SaniCrete STX is a stainless steel reinforced cementitious urethane built to handle thermal shock. While epoxy coatings crack and delaminate under repeated temperature swings, STX flexes with the concrete and maintains its bond. It resists the acids, caustics, and sanitizers used in daily cleaning. And the quartz broadcast surface provides the slip resistance needed in a wet production environment.

At 7,000 square feet, this was a substantial installation. Our crew laid down the full STX system and had the oven room ready for production on schedule.

Phase 2: VR Curb Coating with Cant Cove

We are already coming back for phase 2. The next step is coating the curbs with our SaniCrete VR system and installing cant cove at all wall-to-floor transitions. VR is our vertical and repair grade cementitious urethane, perfect for curb faces, walls, and any vertical surface that needs the same chemical and thermal resistance as the floor.

Cant cove eliminates the 90-degree angle where the floor meets the wall or curb. That sharp corner is where moisture, bacteria, and cleaning chemicals collect. A radiused cant cove creates a smooth, cleanable transition that meets USDA sanitary design standards. It is one of those details that separates a properly engineered food processing floor from one that just looks good on the surface.

20 Years and Counting

Two decades of work speaks louder than any sales pitch. This client has seen our floors perform through years of production, hundreds of washdowns, and multiple facility changes. When he needed flooring for a new plant, he did not shop around. That is the kind of track record we build with every project.
